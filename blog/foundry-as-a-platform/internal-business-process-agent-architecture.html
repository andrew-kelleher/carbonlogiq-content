Architecture overview

The Foundry agent proposes a tool call from the approved catalogue. Read-only calls require scoped permissions. Consequential writes also require human approval before execution.

Approved tools, supported authentication and shared telemetry.

Permitted actions, approval boundaries and failure behaviour.
